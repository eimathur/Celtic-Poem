## About this repo

This is a repo for Celtic poems.

The poems reflect the culture of the seven Celtic nations in traditions, arts, history, or literature. Personal anecdotes of family, studies, or travelling experiences are also great additions.

Because I am of Scottish heritage, I will include poems that reflect the Scottish culture in a variety of ways.
